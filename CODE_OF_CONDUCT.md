# Community Code of Conduct

Project Owner: **SPManchester Private Limited** (https://www.spmanchester.com)

## Community Values
We are committed to providing a professional, respectful, and welcoming environment for all contributors, partners, and users regardless of background or experience level.

## Expectations
- Treat all team members and community participants with professional courtesy.
- Offer and accept constructive technical feedback respectfully.
- Keep discussions focused on software quality, product roadmap, and project objectives.
